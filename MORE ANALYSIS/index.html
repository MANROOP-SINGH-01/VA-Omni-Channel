FORMULA 01

Revenue Realization & Margin

RAW INPUT VALUES
Quantity UnitPrice Discount
↓ HOW IT IS CALCULATED
Gross Revenue = Quantity × UnitPrice
Discount% = (Discount ÷ Gross) × 100
Net Collected = Gross − Discount
↓ FINAL BUSINESS RESULT
₹306.84M Net Realized
85.0% revenue collected (₹54.17M given in discounts)
FORMULA 02

Customer Segmentation (RFM)

RAW INPUT VALUES
Last Order Date (Recency) Total Orders (Frequency) Total Spent (Monetary)
↓ HOW IT IS CALCULATED
Score Recency 1 to 5 (How recently they bought)
Score Frequency 1 to 5 (How often they buy)
Score Monetary 1 to 5 (How much they spend)
↓ FINAL BUSINESS RESULT
6 Customer Groups
Champions (Top Spenders), Loyal, At-Risk, Lapsed...
